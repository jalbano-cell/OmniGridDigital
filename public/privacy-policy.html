1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information in two ways: information you provide to us directly, and information that is collected automatically when you use our Site.

Information you provide

When you fill out our contact or "Free Audit" form, book a strategy call, or otherwise communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • The service or plan you're interested in
  • Any message or details you choose to share with us

If you become a client, we may also collect information needed to deliver our services, such as billing details, business and marketing assets, access credentials to your advertising or analytics accounts, and other information you provide in the course of the engagement.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, and time spent on the Site
  • Referring website or ad campaign

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the free audit or consultation you requested
  • Provide, manage, and improve our web design and advertising services
  • Prepare proposals, quotes, and invoices, and process payments
  • Communicate with you about your project, account, or our services
  • Send marketing communications where you have consented (see Section 7)
  • Measure and improve the performance of our Site and marketing
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues

3. Consent

We collect, use, and disclose personal information with your consent, except where otherwise permitted or required by law. Your consent may be express (for example, when you submit a form or opt in to emails) or implied (for example, where you provide information for a clearly identified purpose). You may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions, by contacting us using the details below. Withdrawing consent may limit our ability to provide certain services to you.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to operate the Site, understand how it is used, and measure the performance of our marketing. These include:

  • Essential cookies required for the Site to function.
  • Analytics cookies, such as Google Analytics and Google Search Console, which help us understand visitor behaviour.
  • Advertising and conversion-tracking cookies, such as those used by Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), and TikTok, which help us and our clients measure ad performance.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the Site functions. Where required, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: with trusted third parties who perform services on our behalf, such as website hosting, analytics, email delivery, scheduling, and payment processing.
  • Advertising platforms: where you are a client, with the platforms we manage on your behalf (Google, Meta, TikTok) as needed to run and measure campaigns.
  • Legal reasons: where required to comply with applicable law, a legal process, or a governmental request, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

7. Email & Marketing Communications (CASL)
